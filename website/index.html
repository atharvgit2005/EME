01 / THE APPROACH

A little unreasonable.
Precisely edited.

Keep the strange idea. Give the mechanics to EME.

EME connects a hands-on video workspace with the IDE you already use. Move a clip yourself, or ask your agent for an edit. The engine turns structured decisions into a timeline you can inspect, revise and render.

01

Bring the material.

Import local video and audio. Cut, trim, move and style clips in Studio. Your source files stay on your device.

02

Explore the possible.

Compare design candidates built from measured typography, spacing and motion. Choose a direction, then make it yours.

03

Make the final call.

Review IDE proposals before applying them. Save explicit preferences for future recommendations, and export through the native engine.

The IDE is the driver.
Your agent describes the edit with structured operations. EME validates timing, sources and track constraints before execution.
The engine holds the craft.
Typography rules, layout measurements and reusable motion parameters give candidates a consistent foundation.
Your taste has a memory.
Explicit comparison feedback informs candidate ranking. Optional TigerGraph integration stores design history and preferences for recall.
The work stays yours.
Direct edits support undo. Agent proposals stay reviewable. Native exports include output checks; browser previews are approximate.
