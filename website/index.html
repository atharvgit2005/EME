The IDE drives. EME executes.+

Your agent sends structured operations. EME checks timing, source files and track constraints before applying an approved plan.

A shared design system.+

Typography rules, layout measurements and reusable motion parameters give every candidate a consistent foundation, across footage and motion graphics.

Your taste has a memory.+

Explicit comparison feedback informs future rankings. Optional TigerGraph integration stores design history and preferences for recall. Applying an edit never counts as a preference vote.

Undo, review, then export.+

Direct edits support undo. IDE proposals remain reviewable. Native exports include output checks; the browser preview is an approximation of the final render.
